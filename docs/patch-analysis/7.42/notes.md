# General
## Global Mechanics
  - Hero Stats
  - Base hero Health increased from 120 to 200
  - Health gained per point of Strength decreased from 22 to 20
  - Magic Resistance gained per point of Intelligence increased from 0.1% to 0.125%  (8 intelligence for 1% magic resistance)
  - Gold and Experience
  - Passive gold per minute no longer scales over game time  (Used to increase from 90 to 111 at 60:00. Now it's a constant 90 gold per minute)
  - Hero kill bounty rescaled  (OLD: 125 + (8 * VictimLevel) + KillstreakFactor | NEW: 100 + (10 * VictimLevel) + KillstreakFactor)
  - Buildings no longer have variable last hit bounties
  - Building bounty decreased
    - Tier 1 tower last hit gold decreased from 110-130 to 100
    - Tier 1 tower gold per player decreased from 90 to 75
    - Tier 2 tower last hit gold decreased from 130-150 to 125
    - Tier 2 tower gold per player decreased from 110 to 100
    - Tier 3 tower last hit gold decreased from 150-170 to 150
    - Tier 4 tower last hit gold rescaled from 170-190 to 175
    - Tier 4 tower gold per player increased from 145 to 150
    - Barracks last hit gold rescaled from 90-135 to 100
    - Melee Barracks gold per player decreased from 155 to 125
    - Ranged Barracks gold per player increased from 90 to 100
  - Roshan gold per player decreased from 135 to 125
  - All heroes now passively receive 50 experience per minute after the starting horn  (Experience is gained even while dead)
  - Total amount of experience needed to reach level 30 increased from 64400 to 66600  (Level -- Old -- New -- Total Increase | 2 -- 240 -- 250 -- 10 | 3 -- 400 -- 400 -- 10 | 4 -- 520 -- 550 -- 40 | 5 -- 600 -- 800 -- 240 | 6 -- 680 -- 850 -- 410 | 7 -- 760 -- 925 -- 575 | 8 -- 800 -- 1050 -- 825 | 9 -- 900 -- 1150 -- 1075 | 10 -- 1000 -- 1225 -- 1300 | 11 -- 1100 -- 1300 -- 1500 | 12 -- 1200 -- 1375 -- 1675 | 13 -- 1300 -- 1450 -- 1825 | 14 -- 1400 -- 1525 -- 1950 | 15 -- 1500 -- 1600 -- 2050 | 16 -- 1600 -- 1675 -- 2125 | 17 -- 1700 -- 1750 -- 2175 | 18 -- 1800 -- 1825 -- 2200 | 19 -- 1900 -- 1900 -- 2200 | 20 -- 2000 -- 2000 -- 2200 | 21 -- 2200 -- 2200 -- 2200 | 22 -- 2400 -- 2400 -- 2200 | 23 -- 2600 -- 2600 -- 2200 | 24 -- 2800 -- 2800 -- 2200 | 25 -- 3000 -- 3000 -- 2200 | 26 -- 4000 -- 4000 -- 2200 | 27 -- 5000 -- 5000 -- 2200 | 28 -- 6000 -- 6000 -- 2200 | 29 -- 7000 -- 7000 -- 2200 | 30 -- 8000 -- 8000 -- 2200 |  | An easy way to approximate the xp needed per level is to use this formula: 0.000976484x^5 − 0.0159378x^4 − 0.247763x^3 + 1.81832x^2 + 132.642x + 147.113 where X is the current level of the hero)
  - Pip Health
  - Standardized rules for units that take a fixed number of hits to destroy and unified the terminology
  - All units are now able to damage units with health bar pips  (* Couriers | * Observer Wards | * Sentry Wards | * Clinkz' Skeleton Archers | * Clockwerk's Power Cogs | * Grimstroke's Phantom's Embrace | * Gyrocopter's Homing Missile | * Juggernaut's Healing Ward | * Keeper of the Light's Will-O-Wisp | * Lich's Ice Spire | * Phoenix's Supernova | * Pugna's Nether Ward | * Radigull's Eggstremism | * Roshan's Banner | * Shadow Shaman's Mass Serpent Wards | * Shadow Shaman's Urnaconda | * Techies' M.A.D. | * Techies' Proximity Mines | * Templar Assassin's Psionic Trap | * Tidehunter's Dead in the Water | * Treant Protector's Eyes in the Forest | * Undying's Tombstone | * Undying's Tombstone Zombies | * Weaver's The Swarm | * Zeus' Nimbus)
    - Attacks from heroes, Roshan, towers, and the fountain deal 1 full pip per attack
    - Attacks from illusions and creeps deal 1/4 pip per attack
  - Some units have a Fragile debuff that causes them to take two pips of damage from melee heroes  (Courier, Templar Assassin's Psionic Trap, Zeus's Nimbus, and Roshan's Banner)

  - Spell Mechanics
  - Leashes that do not expressly pierce debuff immunity (such as Slark's Pounce) will no longer prevent movement-based abilities from being executed even if they are applied after the target becomes debuff immune
  - Abilities that pierce debuff immunity no longer ignore the magic resistance provided by debuff immunity  (* Ancient Apparition: Ice Blast  |  * Batrider: Flaming Lasso  |  * Beastmaster: Primal Roar  |  * Clockwerk: Hookshot  |  * Dark Seer: Wall of Replica  |  * Dawnbreaker: Solar Guardian  |  * Dragon Knight: Elder Dragon Form (Corrosive Breath)  |  * Earth Spirit: Magnetize  |  * Elder Titan: Earth Splitter  |  * Faceless Void: Time Lock  |  * Huskar: Life Break  |  * Lifestealer: Infest  |  * Lone Druid: Entangle  |  * Magnus: Reverse Polarity  |  * Phoenix: Supernova  |  * Primal Beast: Pulverize  |  * Pudge: Dismember  |  * Shadow Demon: Demonic Purge  |  * Spirit Breaker: Nether Strike  |  * Treant Protector: Overgrowth  |  * Vengeful Spirit: Nether Swap  |  * Viper: Viper Strike  |  * Void Spirit: Astral Step  |  * Warlock: Chaotic Offering (Permanent Immolation))
  - Spell Lifesteal from multiple copies of the same item now stack additively (e.g. two Voodoo Masks will provide +30%)  (Previously they did not stack at all)
  - Breakability rules of many innates updated
    - Specifics for this changes can be found in the corresponding ability notes
  - Spell Power Reduction
  - Decreased most item contributions to ability scaling, including Spell Amplification, Area of Effect, and Cast Range
  - Decreased damage of the majority of Magic and Pure damage spells
  - Decreased duration of some long duration slows
  - Specifics for any of these changes can be found in the corresponding ability or item notes
  - Item Potency
  - Attacks from heroes (and hero-like units, including illusions) now have an attribute called Item Potency
  - Some properties of items that apply on-hit effects, like Desolator or Eye of Skadi, are now scaled by the attack’s Item Potency  (For example, the amount of armor reduced by Orb of Blight or the stun chance of Skull Basher)
  - The default Item Potency value for an attack is 100%
  - When debuffs are applied from multiple sources, whichever source had the highest Item Potency will be used
    - Examples
      - Any hero attacking with a Tier 4 Evolved Enchantment has +20% Item Potency, so if they have a Desolator the attack would reduce armor by 7.2  (6 * 120%)
      - Attacks from Pangolier’s Swashbuckle now have 60% Item Potency, so if he has a Desolator the strikes would reduce armor by 3.6  (6 * 60%)
  - Illusions have a default Item Potency of 20% and can now apply most item on hit effects  (Excluding Skull Basher and a few other items. Specific details can be found in the associated item and ability changes)
## Lane Creeps, Towers and Bases
  - Creep Spawn Intervals
  - Number of melee creeps spawned no longer increases at 30:00
  - Now makes Flagbearer creep spawn every wave after the 30:00 mark (instead of every other wave)
  - Time at which 1 Ranged creep is added changed from 40:00 to 35:00
  - Reworked Siege creep spawn count after the 30:00 mark
    - OLD:
      - 0:00-29:59: Every 5 minutes, 1 siege creep spawns per wave
      - 30:00-59:59: Every 5 minutes, 2 siege creeps spawn per wave
      - 60:00+: Every 5 minutes, 3 siege creeps spawn per wave
    - NEW:
      - 0:00-29:59: Every 5 minutes, 1 siege creep spawns per wave
      - 30:00-59:59: Every 2.5 minutes, 1 siege creep spawns per wave
      - 60:00+: Every 2.5 minutes, 2 siege creeps spawn per wave
  - Glyph of Fortification
  - No longer resets cooldown when specific buildings are destroyed
  - Now has 2 charges with a 6 minute replenish time and a 7s cooldown between uses
  - Miscellaneous
  - Couriers now have fragile pip health  (As a part of the comprehensive Pip Health Standardization)
  - Lane creeps now move up to 25% slower if they are pulled out of lane  (Slow builds up over 10s)
    - Pulls near Tier 1 safelane towers don't count and apply no slow
  - Siege creeps no longer switch aggro to the towers that attack heroes
  - Fountain's True Sight radius increased from 1200 to 1800
  - Dire fountain moved ~300 units closer to the Dire Ancient  (Now closer to the distance between Radiant fountain and Ancient)
